# Vehicle Accident Prompt Policy

Focus on immediate next steps after a car accident. Strong hooks:
- "If you were just in a crash, do these three things first."
- "The mistake people make in the first 10 minutes after a collision."
- "What to document before the tow truck leaves."

Visual rules:
- Non-graphic aftermath beats impact simulation.
- Realistic damage, no gore.
- Support footage may show intersection B-roll, hazard lights, or documentation steps.
